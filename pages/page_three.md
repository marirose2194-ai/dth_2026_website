## Which open source project would be useful in your major, and why? 


Since my interests lie in Heritage Management, preservation plays a big role.

Its important that we as future professionals find sustainable and reliable ways to preserve objects.
Since objects are always in transition, modified or lost. Creating a file with its initial footprint the object has the possibility can live on. Another advantage is, that also allow us to study the object a little more closely without compromising its integrity.   

The digital archive as open source is a perfect tool!

- The five checks for Archivematica:

1. Licence: GNU Affero General Public Licence (AGPL-3.0)

2. Last change: 10 November 2026

3. Number of contributors: 60

4. Costumer support: They have a costumer support email where you can report any critical security and safety problems arise (Artefactual. Archivematica, n.d). And they also have active contributors that assist in any type of problems. 

5. Funding: Self-funding and Grants (Invest in Open Infrastructure, 2024) (European Commission, 2024)
