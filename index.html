What are the advantages of open source software?

As the name says, open source, can be accessed by anyone and anywhere if you have access to the internet connection without country imposed restrictions and legislation (The Linux Foundation, 2025). Open source software can offer much more that just a low cost access, but also present a more transparent option, that allows its users to analyse, modify and share source code, and motivating them to collaborate to it. One good example is the software, LibreOffice, that I personally use and where I’m writing this text! As a student, the use of this software is based on personal choice, since I was not a student for five years, I didn’t have access to the Microsoft package, that can be accessed freely when you are a student or I would have to pay to have access, but I still needed a software with similar function and that was familiar in terms of interface and LibreOffice was the most plausible option for me. Also that accessibility to open source software can be great option for independent researches that want to use certain software that is not hidden behind paywalls (LibreOffice, n.d). Open source software, presents it self as a form of attainable digital equality, independently of the economic circumstances of its users, but also acts as a collaborative and learning space for people that have interest in coding and software development. Taking that into account, that open source software are maintain by free and transparent participation – respecting permissions and guidelines of all source codes – we can for sure affirm that, it is a tool made by people for people (Open Source Initiative, 2017) (Wilkins, 2021).

What are the disadvantages of open source software?

Open source software presents various advantages, but like everything it also has it limitations. Yes, open source codes are available to the public, and can be edited within its guidelines, but the maintenance of those projects is quite arduous, what limits technical support. And since not all projects have a dedicated costumer support like LibreOffice, some technical problems can be detected late or take a lot o time to be resolved, or never looked after ever again! One common problem is compatibility, I will use LibreOffice as an example for this one. Since, LibreOffice package is a software similar to Microsoft Office it would be a no brainier for files from both software to work, they do in practice, but not without a couple of dissonances, such as images transference from one model to the other, fonts and formatting. And since, most students use the Microsoft Office package, that allows for live collaboration, what is useful for group work, with LibreOffice that is no possible, unfortunately (The Document Foundation Wiki, 2026). Security, the big word of our times! Is also one of the big disadvantages of open source software, since its code is public, it also is open to potential threats, and since not every project gets the same attention by develops and enthusiasts, some can be hacked (Sentinel One, 2025). It’s Freeee!!! Is it really? Behind every software, exists costs such as: hardware, installation, training, importing data, support and maintenance, are just some examples of actions that can or will require extra costs from you or your company (NiBussinessInfo, n.d) So, before choosing an open source software please be aware of these pros and cons.
